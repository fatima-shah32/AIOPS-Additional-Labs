Lab 04: SysOps System Health Prediction with Time Series Analysis

Objective:
Use time series forecasting to predict future system downtimes or disruptions.

Dataset:
Simulated hourly system logs with system status.

Model Used:
Facebook Prophet

Results:
Average Predicted Downtime: 0.0760
Highest Predicted Downtime: 0.1030
Risk Level: Medium

Files Generated:
- system_logs.csv
- daily_downtime.csv
- downtime_forecast.csv
- system_downtime_forecast.png
- forecast_components.png

Conclusion:
Prophet successfully forecasted future system downtime trends based on historical system logs.
